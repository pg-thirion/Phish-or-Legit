Field reference

SPF, DKIM and DMARC

SPF checks sending infrastructure, DKIM verifies a signed message and DMARC evaluates alignment with the visible From domain. Passing checks do not prove that a request is safe.

Domains and HTTPS

Compare the registered domain with the trusted directory. A valid HTTPS certificate only proves encrypted transport to that host.

Attachments and independent verification

Do not open unexpected executables or active content. Confirm unusual requests through known contact details or the official app. Report suspicious messages through your approved security channel.

Keyboard controls

When enabled, P reports phishing, L marks legitimate, N advances after a verdict, and keys 1 to 4 open evidence tools. Shortcuts are disabled while editing notes or selections.

Privacy and limitations

All cases are fictional. Progress, preferences and session drafts are stored on this browser only. Never enter real passwords, codes, personal information or confidential data in case notes. This is not a live scanner or formal examination.
